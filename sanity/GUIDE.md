# Editing alexisporcaro.com

Welcome! This is how you change anything on your website. You won't need
to touch any code — everything happens in one place.

## The basics

**Your editor lives at:** https://alexisporcaro.sanity.studio
Log in with the same account you used to sign up. Bookmark it.

**Every change follows the same three steps:**

1. Find the thing you want to change in the left sidebar.
2. Edit it.
3. Hit the green **Publish** button at the bottom right.

Within about a minute, the change is live at alexisporcaro.com. There's
nothing else to click — no "deploy," no "rebuild." Publish is the
finish line.

If you've made changes but don't want them live yet, just don't hit
publish. They stay as drafts.

## What's in the sidebar

The sidebar is organized to match the website.

**Single pages** (you'll edit these, you won't add new ones):

- **Site Settings** — your name, role, and contact email
- **Home Page** — everything on the front page
- **About Page** — your biography, photo, facts, and repertoire list
- **Events Page (banner)** — just the photo and title at the top of the Events page
- **Media Page (banner)** — same, for the Media page
- **Lessons Page** — everything on the lessons page
- **Contact Page** — the intro text on the contact page

**Lists** (you'll add and remove these over time):

- **Events** — every recital, concert, and performance
- **Videos** — your video performances
- **Audio Recordings** — your audio recordings

## How to change text

Click a page in the sidebar → click into the field you want to edit →
type → **Publish**. That's it.

A few field types you'll see:

- **Short text** (one line) — for titles, labels, button text
- **Long text** (multiple lines) — for descriptions and intros
- **Rich text** (your bio on the About page) — you can bold things by
  selecting text and pressing **B** or clicking the bold button
- **Lists** (like the repertoire on About) — each line is one item.
  Click the "Add item" button to add more, drag to reorder, click the
  trash icon to remove.

## How to upload your résumé

1. Click **About Page** in the sidebar
2. Find the **Résumé / CV** field
3. Drop in (or click to browse for) a **PDF** of your résumé
4. **Publish**

A "Download Résumé" button automatically appears in the sidebar of
your About page. To take it down, click the field, remove the file,
and Publish again — the button disappears on its own.

## How to change a photo

Click any image field. You'll see:

- **Replace** — upload a new photo (drop a file in or click to browse)
- **Open** — view the current image full size
- **Hotspot/Crop** (icon that looks like a crosshair) — set the focal
  point so the image crops nicely on different screen sizes. Drag the
  blue dot to the most important part of the image. Worth doing for
  any photo with a face.

**Photo tips:**
- High resolution is better — Sanity automatically resizes for the web,
  so you don't need to compress beforehand. Original JPEGs from your
  camera or photographer are perfect.
- For banner images, landscape (wider than tall) works best.
- For portraits and the home page hero, portrait orientation (taller
  than wide) works best.

## How to add an event

1. Click **Events** in the sidebar
2. Click the green **+** button (top right of the events list)
3. Fill in:
   - **Date** — pick the date from the calendar
   - **Title** — the piece or program, e.g. "Strauss Oboe Concerto"
   - **Detail** — ensemble, program details, collaborators, venue
   - **City** — e.g. "New York, NY"
   - **Ticket URL** — paste a link, or leave blank if no tickets yet
4. **Publish**

The site automatically sorts upcoming vs. past events by date. After
an event's date passes, it moves to "Past Performances" on its own —
no action needed.

To **edit** an event, click it. To **delete**, click it then click the
"…" menu in the top right → Delete.

## How to add a video

You'll upload your videos to YouTube (or Vimeo) and then just paste
the link. We don't host the video files themselves.

1. Upload your video to YouTube (set it to Public or Unlisted)
2. Copy the URL from your browser, e.g.
   `https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=abc123`
3. In the studio, click **Videos** → green **+** button
4. Fill in:
   - **Title** — e.g. "Strauss — Oboe Concerto in D, I. Allegro"
   - **Detail** — e.g. "with the Hudson Valley Sinfonia · live, 2026"
   - **Video URL** — paste the YouTube link
   - **Custom Poster** *(optional)* — only if you want a specific
     thumbnail; otherwise YouTube's default is used
   - **Featured?** — turn on if this should be the big video at the top
     of the Media page. Only one video should be Featured at a time.
   - **Order** — lower numbers appear first
5. **Publish**

## How to add an audio recording

Audio works the same way — upload to Spotify or SoundCloud, then paste
the link.

1. Click **Audio Recordings** → green **+** button
2. Fill in:
   - **Title** — e.g. "Mozart — Oboe Concerto in C, K. 314"
   - **Detail** — e.g. "Studio recording · 2026"
   - **Embed URL** — paste the Spotify or SoundCloud track URL
   - **Order** — lower numbers appear first
3. **Publish**

## When something goes wrong

**"I published but the site hasn't updated yet."**
Give it a minute — the site rebuilds in the background. Refresh the
page after about 60 seconds.

**"The site still hasn't updated after a few minutes."**
Hard refresh in your browser: **Cmd-Shift-R** (Mac) or **Ctrl-F5**
(Windows). Your browser may be holding an old version.

**"I made a mistake and want to undo my changes."**
Sanity keeps a full history. With any document open, click the clock
icon (top right) to see previous versions and revert.

**"Something is broken or doesn't make sense."**
Text Michael. Don't try to fix it in the code — that's what he's
here for.
